Request account and cloud data deletion

From the Google email address you use to sign in to Cep Sözlüğüm, email hakankarateke231@gmail.com with the subject “Cep Sözlüğüm — account deletion request”. Ask for your app account, cloud backup and leaderboard records to be deleted. Do not send your password or verification codes.

Scope and timing

Under the current privacy policy, requests to delete your app account, cloud backup and leaderboard records are fulfilled within 30 days. This does not delete your entire Google account. Analytics and crash reports are subject to Google Firebase's retention periods.

Data on your device

Uninstalling the application or clearing its data in Android settings removes local words, statistics and preferences. This is permanent; export anything you wish to keep first. Uninstalling does not automatically delete your cloud backup.

More information

For data categories, retention and your rights, see sections 10–13 of the Cep Sözlüğüm Privacy Policy.
