About this website

This personal portfolio is operated by Hakan Karateke. Contact: karatekehkn@gmail.com.

Visit data

The website has no accounts, contact forms, advertising or analytics cookies. The site owner does not collect browsing data in a database. Until you follow an external link, site resources, including fonts and images, are served from this website only.

Language preference

On your first visit, Turkish is selected if your browser's primary language is Turkish; otherwise English is shown. A language you explicitly choose is stored in your browser's localStorage under hk-language and is not sent to a server. Clear this website's browser data to remove the preference.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP addresses and technical request information to deliver and secure its services. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

External links and contact

Links to Google Play, GitHub, LinkedIn, ResearchGate and Kaggle take you to those services, where their privacy policies apply. Email links open your email application. Information you send by email is used to respond to your request.

Mobile applications

This notice covers the portfolio website only. Read the separate privacy policies for Yansı and Cep Sözlüğüm in the legal section.
